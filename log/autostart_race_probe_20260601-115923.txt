    CLAIM: 'autostart ... causes race conditions with MangoHud'  →  UNSUPPORTED.
      - autostart runs at boot and GATES the UI (ordered Before= the UI service);

    WHAT IS REAL (the correct reason to keep daemons OUT of autostart):
      autostart is SYNCHRONOUS and on the critical chain to the UI. A long-running
      or blocking script dropped there delays UI bring-up. A persistent supervisor
      therefore belongs in its own systemd unit — which is what ETK already does.
